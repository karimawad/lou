Part VQuestions
Where do my tax documents go?

Nowhere. Lou reads your PDFs and photos, does the math and fills the forms inside your browser. There is no account, no server that receives your documents, no cloud text recognition, no AI service and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Who is Lou for?

US citizens, dual citizens and green card holders who lived in Canada for the whole tax year. Lou isn’t built for people who moved between the countries during the year, Canadians with no US status, or snowbirds filing Form 8840. Lou doesn’t do Quebec returns yet.

Will I owe US tax?

Often not. Canadian income tax is usually higher than US tax on the same income, and the foreign tax credit subtracts it from your US tax. US tax most often shows up on income Canada doesn’t tax, such as TFSA earnings or Canadian funds. You still file every year.

Does Lou file my return?

No. Lou prepares the forms; you review, sign and file them. The FBAR is filed by you online through FinCEN, and Lou gives you a worksheet to copy from.

Which Canadian documents can Lou read?

The T1 General and Notice of Assessment, and the T4, T4A, T4A(P), T4A(OAS), T4E, T4RSP, T4RIF, T5, T3, T5008 and T5007 slips. PDFs, CSV exports and phone photos all work. Anything Lou isn’t sure of from a photo is marked “Please check”.

How much does Lou cost?

$49 CAD plus applicable tax, once, for your 2023, 2024 and 2025 returns. You can try it free and pay only when you are ready to download. No subscription, no account.

I’ve never filed a US return. What now?

You’re far from alone. The IRS Streamlined Foreign Offshore Procedures let you file the last three years of returns and six years of FBARs with no penalties if your failure wasn’t willful. Lou can prepare the package. Read Catching up on missed years.
