Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

The short version.

  • Your tax documents and numbers never leave your device. Lou has no server that receives them, no account and no sign-in.
  • We use no analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies.
  • The only things we can see are the ordinary technical logs any website host keeps, plus emails you choose to send us.

Big Time Design and Communication Inc. ("Big Time", "we", "us") makes Lou. This policy explains what happens to information when you use Lou at lou.bigtimedesign.ca.

1. Your tax information stays on your device

When you add documents or type information into Lou, it is read and processed by code running in your own browser. This includes reading PDFs and photos (including text recognition), converting currencies, calculating your return, and filling forms. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Lou does not use cloud text recognition, an AI service or any other outside service to process your information. The app is built to refuse connections to other websites, and you can confirm this in your browser's developer tools.

This includes sensitive details such as names, Social Insurance Numbers, Social Security Numbers, addresses, income, account numbers and balances, and images of your documents. We never receive them. Because we do not hold your information, we cannot look it up, correct it, export it or delete it for you. You can do all of that yourself, as described below.

2. What Lou stores in your browser

  • Your progress. So you can come back later, Lou saves your entries and your document images in your browser's storage on this device. It stays there until you clear it.
  • The app itself. Lou keeps its own program files on your device so it opens quickly and works offline. These files contain no personal information.
  • Backup files you create. If you save a backup (.lou) file or turn on saving to a folder, the files are written to your computer. You can protect a backup with a password. We cannot recover that password.
  • Files you generate. Filled forms, the mapping guide and review packages are created on your device and saved or printed where you choose. They contain your tax information, so store and share them with care.

You can remove everything at any time using "Clear my data from this device" in Lou, or by clearing site data for this website in your browser. Anyone who can use your device and browser profile can see what Lou has saved, so use a device you trust and, on a shared computer, clear your data when you finish.

3. What we can see

  • Server logs. Like every website, the server that delivers Lou's files records technical details of each request, such as IP address, date and time, the page requested, and browser type. This is used to keep the site running and secure. These logs are kept by our hosting provider, Hostinger, for a limited time and do not contain anything you enter into Lou.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to info@bigtimedesign.ca, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to improve Lou. Please do not email us your tax documents or identification numbers. We do not need them to help you.

4. No cookies, no tracking

Lou does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, social media widgets, fingerprinting or any third-party scripts. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

5. Other websites

Lou links to outside sites such as the IRS, the CRA, FinCEN and GitHub. If you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. Lou does not send them anything from your return.

6. Children

Lou is for adults filing their own tax returns. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your rights

Privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, and California's CCPA give people rights to access, correct and delete their personal information. Since we do not collect your tax information, we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete, other than the server logs and emails described above. To ask about those, write to us. If you are in Canada and unhappy with how we handle personal information, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Security

Keeping your information on your device means there is no central store of tax data to be breached. The app is delivered over HTTPS and restricts itself to its own files. The security of your device, browser and backup files is up to you. Use a screen lock, keep your browser up to date, and protect backup files with a password. To report a security problem with Lou, see our security contact.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information, we will say so in the app. Lou will not start sending your tax information anywhere without a clear notice and your choice.
